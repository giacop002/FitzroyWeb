## Overview

The Buen Retiro Copper Project is located in the Atacama Region of northern Chile, approximately 15km from a paved highway and with access to regional power and water infrastructure. The project covers 45km² and is 100% owned by Fitzroy Minerals Ltd.

The project targets a shallow copper-gold porphyry system with an oxide-to-sulphide transition zone that begins at surface in several areas, reducing potential strip ratios and simplifying the metallurgical flow sheet for any future development scenario.

## Geology

The Buen Retiro deposit is hosted within a Late Miocene dacitic porphyry intrusive complex emplaced into Paleozoic basement rocks. Supergene copper enrichment has produced a blanket of oxide and mixed oxide-sulphide mineralisation that extends from surface to approximately 80–120m depth in the main drill-tested areas.

The primary sulphide zone below the supergene blanket contains chalcopyrite and bornite with subordinate molybdenite. Gold occurs as fine electrum associated with quartz-sulphide veinlets and as inclusions in pyrite.

## Exploration History

Fitzroy Minerals acquired the Buen Retiro concessions in 2019. Since then, the company has completed:

- Regional mapping and soil geochemical surveys (2019–2020)
- Induced polarisation (IP) geophysical survey (2021)
- Scout drilling program — 8 holes, 1,850m (2022)
- Infill and step-out drilling — 17 holes, 4,200m (2023–2024)
- Ongoing drill program — 10 holes planned (2025)
