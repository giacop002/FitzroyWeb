Mary has over 25 years of experience in international mineral exploration and human resources management. She has lived and worked in Africa, Europe, and North and South America with Teck Resources and BHP Billiton.

Mary is a graduate in geology from Stanford University and holds a Master's Degree in geology from the University of California, Berkeley. She has served on the boards of several non-profit organizations in the Vancouver area. Mary is also non-executive director for Salazar Resources (TSXV).
