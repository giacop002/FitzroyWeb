Victor has a Bachelor's degree in Geological Sciences, a Master's degree in Energy and Mineral Resources from the University of Texas at Austin, and a CFA charter. He worked as a resources analyst then Portfolio Manager and Chief Investment Officer for United Services Advisors, before spending 11 years at HSBC.

From 2009 he spent nine years as a portfolio manager for Paulson & Co and since 2018 he has been a mining sector consultant, including six years as Director of Strategic Projects for Orion Resource Partners.
