Fitzroy Minerals Ltd. (TSX-V: FTZ | OTC: FTZFF) is pleased to report assay results from drill hole BR-025 at its Buen Retiro Copper Project located in the Atacama Region of Chile.

## Highlights

- 42m at 1.2% CuEq from 38m depth (BR-025)
- Intersection confirms the continuity of the shallow oxide-sulphide transition zone
- Results are consistent with the geological model outlined in the 2024 NI 43-101 Technical Report

## Drill Results

| Hole | From (m) | To (m) | Length (m) | Cu (%) | Au (g/t) | CuEq (%) |
|------|----------|--------|-----------|--------|----------|----------|
| BR-025 | 38 | 80 | 42 | 0.98 | 0.28 | 1.20 |
| BR-025 | 95 | 112 | 17 | 0.61 | 0.12 | 0.69 |

CuEq calculated using Cu US$4.00/lb and Au US$2,000/oz, with 100% metallurgical recovery assumed for resource estimation purposes.

## About Buen Retiro

The Buen Retiro Copper Project is a shallow copper-gold porphyry system located 15km from a paved highway in the Atacama Region of Chile. The project is 100% owned by Fitzroy Minerals.
